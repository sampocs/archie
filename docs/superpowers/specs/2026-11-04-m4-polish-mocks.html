Item actions, copy and the middle tab

What the buttons on an item page should be, what they should say, and how the + tab should look. It starts from how often you'd use each action: anything rare moves into the ••• menu. Newsletters land in the inbox; everything else lands in the queue.

06

Primers, rethought: attached to the item, not queued

Every podcast, article and video already has a primer. Instead of a "Prep me" toggle that sends it somewhere else, the primer becomes the second button: one tap opens it, at any time (before you start, or halfway through). On Today, a queued item that has an unread primer shows a ◆ chip that opens the primer directly.
