Bring in your notes, a couple a day

The 91 Bear notes you marked become Notes items. Each one is searchable and in Ask from day one. Twice a day Today offers one to refresh: you read Archie's write-up, tap Done, and only then do its review cards join your reviews.

Behind the scenes
  • Import: a script on your Mac reads Bear (read-only) and your saved marks, and uploads the 91 notes. Nothing else from Bear leaves the Mac.
  • Write-ups are generated once, at import, in the reading voice you pick from the sampler, so search and Ask are good from day one. Cards are generated at Done, from what your note chose to cover.
  • Order: by topic run, starting from your most recently edited notes; you can reorder or skip like the queue.
  • Later notes: share a Bear note to Archie with the share sheet and it joins the refresher queue. No ongoing Bear sync.
