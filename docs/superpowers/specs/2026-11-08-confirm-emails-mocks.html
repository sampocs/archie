ARCHIE · SIGNUP CONFIRMATIONS

Confirm a newsletter signup

When you subscribe with your Archie address, the newsletter's "confirm your signup" email shows up as a small card at the top of Library, not as something to read. Confirm opens the link; the card goes away once you're back.

How Archie decides it's a confirmation email
  • The email has an https link whose text reads like Confirm signup, Confirm subscription, Verify email, Yes, subscribe me or Activate, and the subject mentions confirming or verifying. A regular issue with a stray "confirm" link doesn't qualify.
  • Gmail's forwarding confirmation is recognised by its sender and subject; its link is in the plain text.
  • Archie's server never opens the link itself. Only you do, from the phone.
