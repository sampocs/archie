Archie · cards you can be graded on fairly

Short card answers

Each card gets a short answer, the thing you're graded on, plus a few lines of detail that show after you answer. Built from the app's real tokens and components; the card content is made up.

1 · Review: what you see after answering

You type "real time stuff". Today the grader compares that with a whole paragraph and can mark you Partly for leaving out the rest. With a short answer it only has to match "Real-time updates". The detail still shows afterwards to jog your memory. I've drawn the detail two ways.

2 · Make cards: proposal, back label, note chip

The proposal shows each card's short answer in full ink and its detail lighter underneath. Opened from a note, the back button says "‹ WebSockets" and goes back to it. "About: WebSockets" becomes a tappable chip. The "Background" label over Archie's one-line reply goes away when the reply sits next to a proposal.

3 · The note, pulled up over the chat

Tapping the About chip slides up the note's write-up (the same sections as its page) without leaving the chat. "See original note ›" swaps in what you wrote. Done or a tap on the backdrop closes it. The chip works in any chat about an item, not only notes.
