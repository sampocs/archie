Archie.
Archie.

Geist Bold, −0.06em tracking, period in forest (mint in dark). Used on the launch screen and outside the app.

Naming rule

  • The name appears only on the home screen, notification title, wordmark, and share-sheet target.
  • Never in UI copy: “Ask about this episode,” tab labeled Ask, “Answer” (not “Archie's answer”).
  • Review dismiss button reads Drop; toast: “Dropped. Fewer cards like this · Undo.”

Color

Graphite chrome, color as signal. Navigation, back, tabs, primary buttons and the Ask bar are graphite (near-white in dark). Color appears only where it means something: domain labels, verdicts, the review card.

Today, primer & graded card

Light is the default; flip the Mode switch for warm-charcoal dark.

Review

Answer in the big text box (or mic), grade appears as a card under the question, Next flicks it away. Drop sits bottom-left of the text box. Use demo: shake to undo after a Drop or Next.

Library & item page

Rows with artwork, List / Domains toggle. Tap any row for the item page with the cover beside the title.

Ask tab

The Ask tab opens to suggested questions (one from today's Pulse). Tap it to stream an answer with a For scale block, [1] citations, source chips with artwork and Make a card, which badges the Review tab.

Ask (chat, in context)

Document style, [1] markers, older turns collapse their sources, follow-ups under the latest answer.

Share sheet

Share from a podcast app → Archie → Finished / Queue it → Save.

Artwork in the mockups is fetched live from the iTunes, Open Library and X image CDNs, the same sources the app uses. Items without art fall back to a monogram tile in their domain color.
