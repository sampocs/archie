The brief as something you read straight through

The brief is a short summary you read top to bottom, not a feed of headlines that each open a full article. Every story is a few complete sentences, so you can read it end to end in about 2 minutes. Going deeper is a small, optional link. Below the stories come "Also happening", "Worth reading" (essays) and "Done". The content comes from the Morning Brew you already have.

Round 201b

Grouped by domain, between B and C

Stories are grouped under their domain. Domains are ordered by their biggest story, so a big politics story puts Politics first and a quiet crypto day falls to the bottom. Within a domain, the most important story comes first. Both versions are full screen like B, and each domain gets a prominent heading.

Where the brief lives, and how it reads

Three shapes. In all three, every story is complete without tapping, and "Sources ›" is the only way into a story.

The end of the brief, and going deeper

The end of the brief: "Also happening" one-liners, "Worth reading" essays as a list, then Done. Then the story page you reach from "Sources ›": a little more depth, and the newsletters it came from.
